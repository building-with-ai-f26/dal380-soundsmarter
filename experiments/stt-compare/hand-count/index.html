How

  • Record each one on the phone's voice recorder, 30 to 60 seconds, in a quiet room.
  • Name the files rec-01.m4a to rec-15.m4a and put them in experiments/stt-compare/recordings/.
  • ALSO IN THE APP means: start the same answer in the app at the same moment, signed in on the local copy, and press Send. That compares the browser's recording with the phone's.
  • Count from the playback. A pause counts if it is a silence you would notice, about half a second or more.
  • For the planted ones, count what you actually said, not what was asked for.
  • When done, press Download hand counts and save the file as experiments/stt-compare/hand-count/hand_counts.csv.

Planted: put these in on purpose

  • rec-07.m4a — Opening filler and prep time ALSO IN THE APP
    Wait about three seconds after pressing record, then open with a long "uhh" before your first word. Carry on normally.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-08.m4a — Soft, quick ums ALSO IN THE APP
    Say about eight ums, each one quiet and short, the kind that is easy to miss.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-09.m4a — A filler sitting at the end of a sentence
    At least five times: finish a sentence, say "um", pause for a second, then start the next sentence.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-10.m4a — Silent pauses in the middle of a sentence ALSO IN THE APP
    No fillers. Five times, stop for a full second in the middle of a sentence, then finish it.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-11.m4a — Silent pauses between sentences only
    No fillers. Five times, stop for a full second after a sentence ends. Keep the middle of each sentence flowing.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-12.m4a — A filler with a pause beside it, and two fillers in a row
    Five times mid-sentence: "um", then a one-second pause. Twice more: "uh um" back to back.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-13.m4a — Repeated words and cut-off words
    Four times, repeat a word ("I I went"). Three times, cut a word off and restart ("the st- the shop"). No ums.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-14.m4a — Other filler sounds and filler words
    Say "er", "erm" and "ah" twice each. Say "like" and "you know" as fillers three times each. No um or uh.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
  • rec-15.m4a — Fast, clean speech: nothing should be counted
    Talk quickly with no fillers and no pauses longer than a breath.
    Prompt: What you did yesterday, start to finish.
